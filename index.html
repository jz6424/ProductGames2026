How to play

You'll play five stories: two local Seattle stories, two national stories and a global story. Each one has five steps.

  1. Read the headline. Each story starts with a short setup and a Yes or No question.
  2. Choose your gut reaction. Answer Yes or No within 20 seconds.
  3. Get more information. See one new piece of evidence about the story.
  4. Keep or change your decision. Then choose your confidence: Leaning, Confident or Certain.
  5. See what happened. Find out the real outcome and your score right away.

More confidence earns more points if you're right, and costs more if you're wrong.

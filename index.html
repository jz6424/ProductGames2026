How to play

Today's broadcast

Five stories: 2 local 2 national 1 global

Each story

  1. Read the question and a short setup
  2. Gut call within 20 seconds
  3. Evidence gives you one new clue
  4. Final call: keep or change it, then pick your confidence
  5. Reveal the real outcome and your score

Two kinds of questions

Yes or NoTap your answer
DialDrag the needle to a number

Scoring

  • Higher confidence wins more if you're right and loses more if you're wrong
  • On a dial, the closer you land, the more you earn
  • Local and harder stories are worth more
